One Ball Win

Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 2, 2026

Overview

One Ball Win is a single-player puzzle game. The app is designed to work without user accounts or in-app purchases, and it uses advertising SDKs to show ads after level results from Level 4 onward.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled by the app and how it is used.

Information We Collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.

The app may process technical information needed for advertising and app functionality, such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers when permitted by App Tracking Transparency, IP address, device model, operating system version, app version, ad placement configuration, ad interaction events, and crash or diagnostic information provided by advertising SDKs.

Local Game Data

The app may store game progress, unlocked levels, star results, hint usage, and audio settings locally on your device. This data is used to provide gameplay features such as continuing progress, selecting levels, and remembering whether music or sound effects are enabled.

Local game progress and audio settings are not used to identify you.

Advertising And Tracking

One Ball Win requests App Tracking Transparency authorization on supported iOS versions. If you allow tracking, advertising partners may use your advertising identifier to deliver and measure ads. If you deny tracking, ads may still be shown, but they should not use your advertising identifier for tracking.

The app may contact advertising configuration and ad delivery services to request ad placement settings, load ads, show ads, and measure ad performance.

  • The app may include advertising SDKs such as Chartboost, Yandex Mobile Ads, Vungle, and InMobi.
  • Advertising partners may process device, ad, and diagnostic information according to their own privacy practices.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Network Access

The core puzzle gameplay can be played without an account. Network access may be used to request advertising configuration, load ads, and support security or fraud-prevention features included in the app template.

Children's Privacy

One Ball Win is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can review the issue.

Data Security

Game progress and settings are stored by iOS on your device. You can remove this local data by deleting the app from your device.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app's features or legal requirements change. If changes are made, the updated policy will be posted with a new effective date.

Contact
